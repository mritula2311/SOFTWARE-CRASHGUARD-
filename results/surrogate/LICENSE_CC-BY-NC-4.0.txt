The files in this folder (fitted summary parameters of the surrogate generator) are derived from the VZCrash dataset (Bianconcini et al., arXiv 2606.06074; Hugging Face vzc-research-chapter/VZCrash, revision 42e1482d005c0e545379d6cfa4939486c9fe4249) and are released under CC BY-NC 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/ , with attribution to the VZCrash authors. The code that fits and samples (src/crashguard/fin_surr.py) is Apache-2.0.
