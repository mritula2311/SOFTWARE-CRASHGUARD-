


How to read it. A row is one preregistered hypothesis; test cells (for example the SF7 and SF9 cells of H-P1a) belong to their row. A hypothesis is called SUPPORTED only if its stated criterion holds and, where the version has a Holm family, the Holm-adjusted p is below 0.05. A p-value below 0.05 with a missed effect size is NOT SUPPORTED. NOT\_RUN rows enter their family with p = 1. PARTLY means some cells hold and others do not. The p-values are the stored ones (\texttt{results/results.json}); where a source gives only 'see REDESIGN\_B\_RESULTS' the value is in \texttt{docs/03\_results/REDESIGN\_B\_RESULTS.md}.
