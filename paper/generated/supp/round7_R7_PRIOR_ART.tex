\subsection*{Round 7b addendum (G2): Chen, Ben-Othman and Mokdad, 2023}

"Denial-of-Service Attacks Against LoRaWAN on MAC Layer" (the title begins with a further word that the repository wording rules ban), IEEE Communications Letters 27(11):3123-3126, 2023, doi 10.1109/LCOMM.2023.3322119. Crossref record checked on 2026-11-16 (authors Mi Chen, Jalel Ben-Othman, Lynda Mokdad). OpenAlex reports the item as closed access with no repository copy, so the full text was \textbf{not reached and nothing was bypassed: abstract only; author to open via the institutional library.}

What the abstract says (OpenAlex): LoRaWAN is exposed to denial of service because of the Aloha MAC protocol and the duty-cycle limitation; two attacks that aim at the confirmed transmission are proposed by investigating the MAC layer; simulation shows that they reduce the packet success rate and the network energy efficiency with fewer attackers than a baseline attack.

Recorded questions that the abstract does not answer: (1) the exact mechanism; (2) whether it drains the duty budget through ACK suppression; (3) whether a defence is proposed. The abstract names no defence. The attack class of round 7 (suppression of acknowledgements against a duty budget) is therefore treated as known, and the paper does not describe it as new; its claim is the reserved-budget defence for a two-stage alert, the bounded model check with its control, and the preregistered results under India's rules.
