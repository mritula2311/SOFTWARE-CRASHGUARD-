\subsection*{Limits}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{No hardware.} The car experiments (H1 to H6) were not run in this repository; the author's hardware logs were not read. Flash sizes are host arithmetic or an x86-64 compile, not ESP32 measurements.
\item \textbf{Simulator-only protocol results.} Delivery, retry and access results come from a simulator with stated assumptions; they are not radio measurements. The Indian 433 MHz rules allow a duty cycle of at most 10\% (G.S.R. 347(E), 2022, read in full on 2026-11-16); the simulations use a stricter 1\% hourly allowance.
\item \textbf{Synthetic coverage by mode.} Synthetic pulses represent only the crash modes that passed the fidelity gate; see \texttt{docs/05\_data\_code/SYNTHETIC\_ASSUMPTIONS.md} and the provenance record \texttt{SYNTHETIC\_PROVENANCE.md} in the same folder, when the release blocks have run. Synthetic data is used for training and clearly labelled stress tests only; validation, calibration, thresholds and real-data metrics use real data only.
\item \textbf{Preregistration timing.} The V4 preregistration addendum was committed before the earliest sealed NHTSA scoring but after one test scoring (P4a); details in \texttt{docs/04\_audits/PREREG\_V4\_AUDIT.md}.
\item \textbf{Data.} VZCrash events were pre-selected on the device and have no vehicle identifier; identical recordings appear across the official splits (\texttt{docs/04\_audits/VZCRASH\_AUDIT.md}). NHTSA records are mostly high-speed barrier tests.
\item \textbf{Security.} One shared key, no key rotation, no encryption of position; limits are listed in \texttt{docs/01\_protocol/PACKET\_FORMAT.md}. The alert-protocol model check is a bounded search of a model, not a proof of the firmware.
\end{itemize}
