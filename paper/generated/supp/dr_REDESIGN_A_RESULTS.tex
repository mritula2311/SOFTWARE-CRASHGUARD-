\subsection*{4. What D2 cost and changed in the burst cells (descriptive)}

Gateway airtime spent on cluster ACKs: 7.5 s per simulated hour (D1 has none: 0.0). Vehicle airtime share of the allowance: D1 0.167, D1+D2 0.164. Collided share of alert transmissions: D1 0.451, D1+D2 0.448. A 64-byte signature makes each cluster ACK about as long as an alert, and the gateway cannot receive while it sends, so a large part of the benefit of earlier stops is paid for in gateway deafness (inferred from these counts; not isolated).

\subsection*{6. Reading with care}

\begin{itemize}
\item These are results of the custom simulator only. The cross-simulator check (ns-3 lorawan, X1) is run separately; until D1 is also checked there, D1 is not placed in the main paper and its result is described as simulator-dependent in principle.
\item The severity table is a weak baseline in this simulator (it waits 5 s before the initial Critical send and retries without spacing). The gain over the best of six baselines is small (mean +0.03; the smallest cell is slightly negative but within the bound). Most of D1's gain over the table is also available from quick retries with a spreading-factor raise; D1 adds a duty-cycle budget, a load index and a loss estimate, and the check against quick retries is in section 3.
\item D1's table uses the simulator's own spreading-factor decay (0.7 per step). A real channel may differ; the ns-3 check tests another physical model.
\item Absence statements: no study with a gateway-signed aggregated ACK for LoRa alert bursts was found in the sources checked (web search; IEEE Xplore, Scopus, Google Scholar and TRID not run); \texttt{docs/06\_references/CLOSEST\_PRIOR\_WORK.md}.
\item DEV and FRESH-A share the finite pool of real class pairs and decision times (addendum A, 5f). The FRESH-A grid is new in arrivals, channel draws, geometry and cell parameters.
\end{itemize}
