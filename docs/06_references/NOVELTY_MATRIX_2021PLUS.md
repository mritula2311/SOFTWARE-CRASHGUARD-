## How to read this

- Judged against journal articles with publication date 2021-01-01 or later, found with the OpenAlex queries in `LIT_QUERIES_2021PLUS.md` (13 topics, 26 queries, 2026-11-14). Items were read at abstract level through the OpenAlex record; no full text was read in this run.
- **Sources not run:** Scopus, Google Scholar and TRID (not available). IEEE Xplore (Journals 2021 to 2026) returned HTTP 418 to a scripted request and was not bypassed, so it is NOT_RUN in this session; Xplore results in `LITERATURE_STATUS.md` sections 5 and 8 come from the author's earlier browser run.
- **Absence wording, allowed only in this form:** "no journal article from 2021 onwards found in the sources checked (OpenAlex and IEEE Xplore; Scopus, Google Scholar and TRID not run)". Where the Xplore part of that sentence rests on the earlier browser run and not on this session, the matrix says so in the row.
- Conference papers and pre-2021 work are context and are still cited where closest: Nessa et al. (CNS 2020), Tamang et al. (MSWiM 2023), Wesemeyer et al. (EuroS&P 2020), Dong et al. (ISSRE 2024), Khan et al. (DCOSS 2019), Farha et al. (IEEE TMC, 2020-01-01 on the record).
- Overlap levels: none found, partial, substantial. Rule: substantial overlap means the claim is dropped or the prior method becomes a baseline.
- **Row keys.** D1, R1 to R4 and T1 to T3 follow the project documents (`PREREGISTRATION_V11.md`, `V12.md`, `PREREGISTRATION_V14.md`). C1 to C7 are the contributions of the combined paper (file 2, finalised 2026-11-15); the descriptors used before that date (integrated system, conformal severity, synthetic generators) are kept below as rows N1 to N3, because they remain negatives or supporting results.

## Further results of this check

- **Aggregated ACKs exist.** DG-LoRa (Sensors 2021) proposes group acknowledgment transmissions in a time-synchronised LoRa frame. D2 (gateway-signed cluster ACK) is already a negative result; any text on cluster ACKs must cite DG-LoRa and drop any claim of uniqueness. Overlap with D2: substantial for the aggregation idea.
- **Overhearing and suppression (topic 6):** query 6.1 returned 0 hits. No 2021+ journal article found on overhear-and-defer with LoRa ACKs in the sources checked.
- **Simulator validation (topic 8):** Citoni et al. (IEEE Sensors J. 2022) compare a real urban LoRaWAN deployment with simulation; it is the calibration anchor for the ns-3 cross-check and for the T1 limits. Magrin et al. (TWC 2021) is the analytical reference for single-gateway LoRaWAN.
- **Survivability (topic 13):** the two queries returned 4 and 0 hits, none relevant. T4 therefore rests on datasheets, not on a prior study; no novelty claim.
- **Name clash:** a 2022 journal paper titled "LoED: LoRa and Edge Computing based System Architecture for Sustainable Forest Monitoring" is unrelated to the Imperial dataset "The LoRaWAN at the Edge Dataset". Cite the dataset by its arXiv number (2010.14211).
- **Titles without abstracts in the record** (Zorbas 2023; AACN for children 2022; Performance Evaluation 2024; Accid. Anal. Prev. 2021 kinematic-feature algorithm): title and metadata only; they are not used as support for any statement about content.
