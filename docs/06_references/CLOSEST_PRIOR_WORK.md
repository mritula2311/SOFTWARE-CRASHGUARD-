## Reading for D1 and D2
- **D1 (per-severity deadlines, duty-cycle budget, online loss estimate, table solved offline):** each piece has a neighbour in the literature (retry limits and timeouts in the LoRaWAN rules and Khan et al.; link-quality-driven SF choice in ADR papers; urgency-aware retransmission possibly in the 2020 mines paper). The combination in one per-event retry table, tested against the severity table and five baselines, is what D1 offers. **No claim is made that it is unique**; a paper that already does this would have to be implemented as a baseline, and the 2020 mines paper cannot be checked until its text is read.
- **D2 (gateway-signed cluster ACK listing the vehicles received, stop only if listed, defer otherwise, gated by load):** no study with a gateway-signed aggregated ACK for LoRa alert bursts was found in the sources checked (web search only; IEEE Xplore, Scopus, Google Scholar and TRID not run). This is not a claim that none exists.
- Items for the author to open by hand: the 2020 CNS mines paper, the 2023 CCNC paper, the IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2025 paper, and the MSWiM 2023 paper in full.
