Reading. The side rule keeps 0.90 or more under every tilt up to 30 degrees, under noise x4, bias sd 0.08 g, jitter, dropped samples and crash peaks down to a 3 g median (exactly 0.900 at 3 g). It fails under the two axis-sign flips (front and rear swap under an x flip, left and right under a y flip: accuracy near 0.5) and at 10 Hz sampling (accuracy 0.26: a 12 to 45 ms pulse is mostly missed). The trigger alone has a false-alarm rate of 0.6 to 0.7 on these bench non-crash events (ramp bumps, rough driving and some hand taps exceed 2 g), so the trigger cannot separate crash from non-crash here; the forest of the transfer test rejects most bench pulses (recall 0.07 to 0.5): bench pulses are 12 to 45 ms against 80 to 150 ms for vehicle crashes, so that test checks plumbing and scale handling, not real transfer.

## S3: end-to-end latency (H-S4), supplied H7 file

Median trigger-to-ACK of 96 first-try deliveries: 519 ms (p95 527 ms); all ACKed events: median 519 ms. Decomposition of the median first-try event: post-trigger window 250 ms + compute 11 ms + wait before transmit 15 ms + alert airtime 169 ms + base turnaround 12 ms + ACK airtime 62 ms. ANALYTICAL lower bound before compute and waiting: 250 + 169.2 + 61.7 = 480.9 ms. Each retry adds about 600 ms. A "sub-500 ms" statement therefore fails for this window, alert and ACK at SF7 unless the post-trigger window is shortened or the ACK is not waited for; this is a statement about the model, not the radio.

## S5: IMU only against IMU plus SW-420 confirmation (H-S6)

Rule: at least 2 of 6 switches within -100 to +300 ms of the trigger. Pooled over the 15 sensor variants (1,500 crash and 1,500 non-crash events). Exact McNemar test on the non-crash events (the discordant pairs all go one way).

Reading. With the base switch model the confirmation removes 0.29 of the false alarms but costs 0.043 of the crash recall (weak crashes do not shake enough switches), so the preregistered criterion fails on the recall loss. With low switch thresholds (0.8 to 2.0 g) it costs no recall but removes only 0.032; with high thresholds (3.0 to 6.0 g) it removes 0.57 and costs 0.27 of the recall. Any gain therefore depends on the simulator's assumption about switch sensitivity; a real SW-420 result needs the author's logs.

Per-variant values are in `results/tables/sh_s5_variants.csv`.

## S4: security (H-S5)

One number: the repository's suite has 58 cases (48 base, 10 extension), all 58 behave as expected; the cases are listed once in `docs/03_results/SIMHW_S4_CASES.md`. 30 and 38 are not found in any current file or key. Counter behaviour: the reference verifier persists its counter (yes: an older alert after a base reboot is not accepted); a RAM-only verifier accepts it (yes), which is the S06 finding of the simulated model; a car whose counter restarts is rejected (yes), the S08 availability cost. The ESP32 firmware is not in this repository, so what it implements is NOT_RUN. The documented gaps stay: a base that loses its counter accepts a recorded alert again, and an alert for another node id signed with the shared key is accepted; R1 and R4 address them.
