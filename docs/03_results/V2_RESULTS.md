## Not run, and what the studies do not show

- **Study C (combination)** is NOT_RUN. It was conditional on a positive result in A or B that could supply a ranking score. B has no positive result, and the only positive result in A is the point estimator, which gives no interval width; the A5 interval widths failed H-A2. In addition, in the per-car framework each car has at most one competing escalation, so a ranking score has nothing to rank.
- A deterministic upper bound with the stated containment, adaptive intervals that are narrower where clipping is mild, a clip-onset flag that saves 100 ms or more, load-adaptive escalation that gains 3 points, and a tag-length gain of 2 points or more were not found.
- Nothing here was measured on a car, an ESP32 or radios.
