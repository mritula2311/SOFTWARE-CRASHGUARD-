## 1. Retry policy of the shipped node (read from code)

Per event one FIFO queue: the flash (14 bytes, at most 4 tries), then the confirm (97 bytes, or the 13-byte micro-confirm after an acknowledged flash; at most 4 tries) or three unconfirmed cancels 0.3 s apart. Each confirmed try is followed by the receive windows (0.32 s in ns-3). Nothing is withdrawn when the full decision is ready and no frame type has priority; a frame waits for the duty model with no deadline. Heartbeats are unconfirmed, so ACK suppression does not touch them. The firmware has no two-stage queue; the policy exists in `xsim/ns3/xsim_alert.cc` (r3t, r6m) and in the trace replay.

## 4. Bounded formal check (FORMAL)

Model of `src/crashguard/r7_formal.py` (independent integer re-implementation): steps as time units, flash cost 1, confirm cost 4 (k tries, k in {1, 2}), cancel 3 copies, reserve R = 4k + 1, trailing window of 30 steps (hourly model) or off-time 3 x cost (T model), B in {16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 40}, up to 2 earlier confirm events and the target (confirm or cancel), the attacker choosing every ACK outcome, gaps of 0, 2 or 5 steps. An earlier run with a window of 10 steps was replaced before reporting: an event lasts about 13 steps, so earlier events never shared a window and the unconditional form could not fail. The bounds differ from V18 (B 16 to 40; at most 2 earlier events).

Invariant 1 (class-L frames never leave less than the reserve; the check is run on the log, not on the admission code) and Invariant 2 conditional on the reserve being intact hold in the bounds. The unconditional form fails for the reserved policy only with 2 earlier events (smallest case: 2 earlier events, B = 16, k = 2, model H; the trace is in `results/tables/r7_formal.json`): the earlier follow-ups have spent the reserve. This is the same limit as in the simulation. FORMAL here means exhaustive over the stated bounds, not a proof for all sizes.

The spreading factor at which the two-stage alert is enabled does not move: 0 steps under H1, H10 and T10 (SF7). The load gate does not depend on the reserve; the lockout gate is the same inequality (the node admits the flash when s_f + A_f / d <= T_F, and not otherwise), now enforced by the node per event, and the reserve limits the number of flash tries (3, 2, 1, 0 at SF7 to SF10 under the off-time). At SF11 and SF12 the reserve for one event takes 25% and 47% of a 1% allowance, a cost the old policy does not have.

## 7. Limits

- No result here is measured on hardware or on a commercial gateway. The attack is a model of ACK suppression with a stated attacker; selective jamming of gateways is shown to be feasible in the literature (BlindSpot), not here.
- Exhausting the allowance needs sustained triggers (one every about 85 s at SF7 and 1%); the rate at which a detector produces such triggers was not measured.
- n* counts the shipped caps; a node with a different cap changes it.
- The preregistered reserve (one event) protects the next follow-up only while the reserve is intact; the sized reserve costs airtime.
- Benign cost: SF7 only.
