## Plain summary

- **Block P (physics generators).** No generator passed the realism gate (point AUC at most 0.60): the two physics generators and the earlier family generator, refit on the development fold, are all separable from real pulses in every mode (`docs/03_results/PH_INTEGRITY_REPORT.md`). The VZCrash-style surrogate also fails (discriminator AUC 0.995 [0.994, 0.995]). Under the preregistered rule no synthetic row may train the confirmatory reruns.
- **Block Q (reruns).** With no gated synthetic data, the confirmatory reruns are the real-only reruns. They give the same verdicts as the original studies: nothing changed. Synthetic arms that were run anyway are EXPLORATORY and labelled "gate failed".
- **Block A (protocol).** Neither candidate passed on the held-out grid: H-A1 (OS-DR) not supported, H-A2 (GS-CA) not supported, H-A3 not supported (a forged event-level ACK from a key holder stops every vehicle of the cell). Both candidates go to the negatives register.
