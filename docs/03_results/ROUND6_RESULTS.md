## 2. What each part shows

- **R6-1 micro-confirm** (`ROUND6_R1_MICRO.md`). ns-3 airtime is 0.46 to 0.77 of R3's with delivery within 0.003. The extra-airtime test against single-stage is nearly automatic. The frame has 13 bytes in ns-3 (design: 7) and the trace delivery test is zero by construction. The micro-confirm gives up the content of the full confirm.
- **R6-1c, R6-3 formal** (`formal/`, `formal/out/r6/`). ProVerif and Tamarin prove the binding, anti-replay and cancel lemmas of the micro-confirm; the control model whose tag omits T1 is attacked in both. The hash ratchet gives authenticity and secrecy of past alerts within a bound of 3 ratchet steps (the bound is part of the result). The bounded quarantine model finds an attack on the count-before-verification rule (2 frames from a vehicle without any key); the count-after-verification rule holds by construction (frames that fail verification do not reach the counters), so the content of H-R6-3b is the attack on the other rule and the fix.
- **R6-2 sequential exit** (`ROUND6_R2_SEQUENTIAL.md`). Mean time gain 14.2 ms (lower limit 9.0 ms); retraction share +0.126 (upper limit 0.143). Not supported; a regime boundary: the fixed rule already decides at the earliest checkpoint for most events.
- **R6-4 footprint** (`ROUND6_FOOTPRINT.md`). Forest data 15,771 bytes; linked flash 33,559 bytes (unused functions included); micro-confirm check stack chain 1568 bytes (static sum). Emulator counts and energy NOT_RUN (no emulator).
- **R6-5, R6-7 bounds and propositions** (`ROUND6_BOUNDS.md`, ANALYTICAL). Exact airtime and attempt bounds, the extra-airtime lower bound (0.336 at SF7, 0.380 at SF9), a greedy-retry proposition with proof, the spreading-factor rule, and the quarantine indexing finding.
