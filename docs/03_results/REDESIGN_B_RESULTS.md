## 2. D3 (gated slotted retries), FRESH-B, next to the original GS-CA failure

**D3 is a negative result** (kill rule of V12 10.2: the 5-point gain is not met; the no-harm part holds). The gain is real in sign (interval above zero) and about 80 times smaller than required. **Mechanism seen on the DEV grid (not on FRESH-B):** in a DEV burst hour the slots were used 2.1 times in total for 25 burst alerts with up to 12 retries each, because a vehicle rarely decodes two other vehicles in this channel model (vehicle-to-vehicle links are mostly out of range), so a burst-size estimate without an oracle is rarely available. GS-CA and the Tamang-style baseline both used a burst size given by an oracle. This reading is inferred from counts, not isolated by an ablation. Design deviations that may matter are in V12 10.6 (the gate counts other vehicles; slots apply to the flash of a round; the 5 s epoch cap does not hold at SF12).

The statement allowed in the paper: "the firmware decision path was executed on simulated hardware-format inputs in a host build; an ESP32 emulator and a physical device were not used; no measurement on physical hardware is reported."
